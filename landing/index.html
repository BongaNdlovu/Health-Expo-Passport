What your team gets

Built for the way a church runs an expo

Admins, team leaders, desk staff and volunteers each see what their job needs, and nothing else.

Your stations, your order

Choose which stations run and the route visitors walk. Every desk and the big screen follow it.

Volunteers, organised

Members pledge from their own phones; admins and team leaders place them at stations, and they earn service badges.

Follow-up that sticks

Urgent readings, prayer requests and 48-hour calls in one place, ticked off as they are attended to.

Plans rooted in the health message

Home plans built on Ellen White's counsel, with the references shown. Admins can write their church's own plans too.

A big screen for the room

Put the live board on a TV: who is being served and who is next at each station, by badge number only.

Each church on its own

Every church sees only its own people. Your admins approve who joins, and each role sees only its own tools.

Installing

Careful with people's data

Health information, handled with care

Built around POPIA's rules for personal health information. The notice each visitor reads says exactly this.

Readings don't linger

Health readings and prayer requests are deleted 7 days after the visit. Only visitors who agree to follow-up keep their name and contact details on file, for up to 2 years.

Each church's data is separate

Your people, your expos, your queue. No other church can see them.

Yours to export

Visitor CSV, volunteer roster and prayer list, any time. Your data is never held hostage.

No ads, no trackers

Nothing is sold and nothing is shared with advertisers. Consent is recorded for every visitor.
