## Root causes

Three causes account for 14 of the 33:

1. **A session that ends by itself resets less than a deliberate sign-out** (A-1, A-2/B-2, A-3, A-4, A-6). This is
   step 4 in `STATE-MACHINES.md`: one reset used by both paths.
2. **Async helpers write after an `await` without checking the session generation** (B-3, B-4, C-1, D-1, D-3, A-5).
   `cloudSync` and `cloudPush` do this correctly; the helpers around them do not.
3. **The database trusts a phone-written value or leaves a rule to the client** (G-1, G-2, G-3).
